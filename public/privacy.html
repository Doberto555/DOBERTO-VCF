PRIVACY & GOOGLE USER DATA

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information DOBERTO VCF accesses, how Google user data is used, when it may be processed by service providers, how it is protected, and how it is retained or deleted. Google Contacts access is optional and is requested separately from Google Sign-In.

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to DOBERTO VCF and its use of Google Sign-In and Google Contacts. It also covers information you submit directly when joining the DOBERTO VCF network.

2. Information we access or collect

  • Information you provide: name, country, WhatsApp number and email address submitted during registration.
  • Google Sign-In information: your Google account identifier, display name, verified email address and profile picture.
  • Google Contacts authorization data: OAuth access/refresh tokens, granted-scope metadata, connection status and synchronization timestamps/errors needed to operate the optional Contacts feature.
  • Google Contacts scope: DOBERTO VCF requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts because the production feature creates DOBERTO VCF network contacts in the authenticated user's Google Contacts account.
The current production workflow does not use the Google Contacts scope to download, sell or build a copy of the user's existing Google address book. It uses Google People API to create the contacts selected by the DOBERTO VCF synchronization workflow.

3. How we use Google user data

  • Authenticate the user and maintain a secure DOBERTO VCF session.
  • Associate the user's DOBERTO VCF registration with the correct Google account.
  • When the user explicitly enables Google Contacts, create DOBERTO VCF network contacts in that user's Google Contacts account.
  • Maintain optional Auto-Sync, synchronization status, retry/error handling and account-level controls.
  • Protect the service, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures and provide user support.

Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling for advertising, credit decisions, or unrelated purposes.

4. Google Contacts functionality and least-privilege use

Google Contacts permission is separate from basic Google Sign-In. DOBERTO VCF requests it only after an authenticated user chooses to connect Google Contacts. The requested write-capable scope is necessary because the feature creates contacts in the user's Google Contacts account. The application does not request this permission merely to sign the user in.

5. Sharing, transfer and disclosure

  • We do not sell, rent or trade Google user data.
  • We do not share Google user data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.
  • Google user data may be processed by infrastructure/service providers strictly as needed to operate DOBERTO VCF, such as hosting/serverless infrastructure, database storage and transactional email services. These providers receive only the information required for their service role.
  • Requests to Google People API necessarily transmit the data required to create the requested contact in the user's own Google account.
  • We may disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect users, the service or the public from fraud, abuse or security threats.

6. Data protection and security

  • Google OAuth tokens are kept server-side and are not exposed in the public client application.
  • Stored Google OAuth tokens are encrypted using authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption before being written to the database.
  • HTTPS/TLS is used for production traffic and calls to Google APIs.
  • Application secrets and privileged database credentials are kept in server-side environment configuration rather than public frontend code.
  • Administrative access controls, security headers, request-size limits and rate limiting are used to reduce unauthorized access and abuse.

No system can guarantee absolute security, but DOBERTO VCF applies reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the data it processes.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Google profile information and DOBERTO VCF registration data are retained while needed to provide the service or until the user requests deletion, subject to legitimate legal or security requirements.
  • Encrypted Google OAuth credentials and Google Contacts connection metadata are retained while the Google Contacts integration remains connected or until the user requests server-side deletion.
  • If a user revokes DOBERTO VCF access in their Google Account, the revoked Google credential can no longer be used to access Google APIs. A user may also request deletion of the stored connection record and associated Google user data.
  • After a verified deletion request, DOBERTO VCF will delete or de-identify the requested Google user data and stored OAuth credentials from active systems within 30 days, except information that must be retained for legal, fraud-prevention or security purposes.
  • Contacts previously created in the user's Google Contacts account remain under that user's control. Revoking DOBERTO VCF does not automatically delete those contacts; the user can edit or delete them directly in Google Contacts.

8. User controls and revocation

Users may disable Auto-Sync in DOBERTO VCF at any time. They may revoke DOBERTO VCF's Google access from their Google Account permissions page. Revocation stops future authorized Google API access. For deletion of DOBERTO VCF server-side records, users can contact us using the address below.

Manage Google Account permissions

9. Notifications and non-Google data

If you provide an email address or enable browser notifications, DOBERTO VCF may send service-related messages such as VCF availability, account status or important operational updates. Notification permissions can be disabled at any time.

10. Google API Services User Data Policy

DOBERTO VCF's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
